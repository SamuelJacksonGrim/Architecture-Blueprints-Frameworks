### D-011 — Workspace, not pipeline

**Question:** Does the ten-artifact language serialize cognition?

**Chosen:** No. The artifacts are a network. `depends_on` and `order` constrain
when something may be marked `complete`, not when it may be thought or drafted.
One pass may traverse the whole space. Revising an earlier artifact is reasoning.
Evidence is for claims and consequential decisions, not permission to continue.
Cognitive work and observable output are different granularities. PIPELINE.md
keeps its name and loses its queue.

**Rejected:** An eleventh artifact. A checkpoint protocol. A turn-per-file rule.
Treating a longer transcript as more rigor.
