# QUICKSTART

**You don't need to know how to code.**

---

## 1. Say what you want

Plain English. Vague is fine:

> *"Build me an app that tracks when I last watered each of my houseplants."*

You are not asked to design it. You do not approve each file.

## 2. Point an AI at this repo

> *"Build the plant-watering app using this repo. Read AGENTS.md first."*

[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) is the entity's front door. It will not stop after
each artifact for your signature.

## 3. Look at what it shows you

You get a project, a plain-English summary, and an honest note about whether
the main path ran. You keep authority: accept, reject, or redirect.

---

Left alone, an AI vibe-codes. This repo gives it a language so the design is
inspectable and only as deep as the thing needs. The AI still has to think —
in as few passes as the work takes. You still get to say no.
